Getting started

1. Signing in

OmniReach places outbound telephone calls that are handled by an AI voice agent. You provide the people to call and what the call is about. The platform dials each person, holds the conversation, records what was agreed, and writes the outcome back to your own records.

Signing in

There is no password. Enter your work email address and a six-digit code arrives in your inbox within a few seconds. Enter the code and you are in. Each code is valid for ten minutes and can be used once.

Your browser is remembered afterwards, so you will not be asked for a code every day. Signing out from the profile menu, top right, showing your initial, clears that.

Creating an account

Choose Create an account on the sign-in screen. What happens next depends on your email domain.

Your email domainWhat happens
On the approved listYou register, receive a code, and sign in immediately. No approval is required.
Approved individually, by addressThe same, and it continues to work even when open sign-up is switched off.
Any other domainYou are told the request has gone for review, and it is added to the Access requests queue. Nothing on the platform is reachable until it is approved.
If you see the message "sent to an administrator for review", the platform is behaving correctly. Contact your Streebo representative, and approval normally takes a minute.
Getting started

2. Setting the company

At the top of every screen there is a marker reading operating as, followed by a company name. That is the agent that will place your calls, and it shapes everything that follows.

To change it, open Company Profile. The quickest route is to load one of the industry presets and then edit the few fields that matter for your prospect: the company name, the country, and the agent's own name.

What the profile controls

  • Which call types are available. Only the use cases enabled in the active profile appear on the Single Call screen. A clinic profile offers appointment confirmations and results; it does not offer loan collections.
  • How the agent sounds. Voice, language, and how it addresses people: first name, "Mr" or "Ms" plus surname, first name plus "ji", or "sir" and "ma'am".
  • Money and dates. The currency, and whether amounts are grouped in the Indian style (25,00,000) or the Western style (2,500,000). Amounts are spoken as words, so this affects what the customer hears.
  • The compliance framework the agent works under, which governs what it will and will not say.
Set the profile before launching, not during. A campaign uses the profile as it stood at the moment it was launched, for every call in that run. Edits made while a campaign is running take effect on your next campaign.

3. Simulation and live calling

At the top right of every screen there is a SIMULATION switch. It is the most important control on the platform.

A simulated call runs the same conversation logic and produces the same record, including the actions the agent would have taken during the call. Every simulated record is marked Simulated in the call history and is counted separately wherever figures are reported.

Use simulation for everything except the moment a real telephone needs to ring. Building a profile, checking that a script reads well, testing a file, demonstrating the analysis table, showing the write-back columns: none of this requires telephony.
Making calls

4. Placing a single call

Use Single Call to place one call at a time, either to hear the agent work or to demonstrate one particular conversation.

  1. Choose a use case. These are the call types enabled in the active profile. Each is a complete conversation, not a single scripted sentence.
  2. Enter the customer details. Name and telephone number. The number must be in international format, beginning with a plus sign and the country code, for example +919820011001 rather than 9820011001. Time of day is filled in automatically.
  3. Complete the call variables. These change with the use case, and required fields are marked with an asterisk. A bill reminder asks for the product, the amount and the due date; an appointment confirmation asks for the type, the date and the time.
  4. Launch the call.
Leave a field blank rather than entering a placeholder value. The agent speaks around an empty variable, so an unknown balance simply does not come up in conversation. A substitute figure will be spoken to the customer as though it were correct.

5. Calling from a list

Smart Targeting turns a file of contacts into a queue of calls. Upload the export exactly as it comes out of your system. The columns do not need renaming.

What the file must contain

A name and a telephone number, somewhere in the file. Everything else determines which call is placed and what is said during it, but a row without a name or a number cannot be called and will be held back.

Columns are matched in three ways, in order:

  1. By name, including the many variants in common use: msisdn, consignee, appt_dt, guardian_phone, Bill Amount, Payment Due On.
  2. By meaning, where a longer heading still describes the field: bill_due_date, restoration_eta, pending_documents.
  3. By the values themselves, where the heading is uninformative. A column named crm_field_7 containing numbers in the form +9198… is recognised as the telephone number.

File formats handled

Comma, semicolon and tab separated files; files saved with a byte-order mark; a report carrying its own title on the first line; headings that include units, such as Amount (INR). Values altered by spreadsheet software are also recovered, including telephone numbers converted to scientific notation and dates stored as serial numbers.

Sample files

Two are available on the Smart Targeting screen.

  • Download CRM Template uses the platform's own column names. Useful as a starting point when someone asks what the file should look like.
  • Download Sample Export is shaped like a partner's own export: unfamiliar column names, no use-case column, missing values, and several kinds of call in one file. It can be uploaded unchanged.

Other sources

Under Data source, contacts can also be drawn from a URL (CSV or JSON), a published Google Sheet, or a REST API. Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho and a direct database connection use the same interface and require credentials before they can be enabled.

6. Reading the analysis

Once a file is analysed you are shown two things: a summary of how your columns were interpreted, and a row-by-row table of what will happen.

How your file was read

A list of pairs, such as Mobile No → Phone number and Account Holder → Customer name. Check this first, as everything below it depends on this interpretation. Any column that was not used is listed at the end.

The analysis table

ColumnWhat it shows
OrderThe dialling order. Urgent calls are placed first: service disruptions, then arrears, payment reminders, appointments, documents, renewals, feedback, leads, and marketing last.
Use CaseThe call this row will receive.
ConfidenceHow certain the match is, and what would otherwise have been chosen. "Named in file" means a use_case column specified it directly.
Why this callThe evidence in plain words: "payment due in 5 days", "23 days overdue", "documents outstanding".
What we'll sayThe values the agent will speak to that person.
StatusReady, or the reason the row was held back.

Why rows are held back

Reason shownMeaning
no phone number
no customer name		The row is missing one of the two fields required to place any call. The message names which.
missing Deadline, or another fieldThe row matches a call type but lacks something that call cannot proceed without. Add the named column and upload again.
do-not-call (data)A suppression column in your file. Recognised spellings include Opted Out, DNC, Do Not Contact, unsubscribed and Consent Revoked.

A row may also report that nothing on it matches the calls this agent makes. This occurs when the active profile has no call type for what the row describes, such as a file of amounts and due dates uploaded against a clinic profile that only confirms appointments. Change the profile or the file.

Date format warning. If every date in a column falls on or before the 12th, the file does not indicate whether dates are written day-first or month-first, and an amber note appears. Dates are read day-first, so 09/11 is the 9th of November. If the file originated in an American system it means the 11th of September, and the dates on those rows will be wrong. A single date after the 12th anywhere in the column resolves this and the warning disappears.

7. Launching a campaign

Move to Bulk Campaign. You will see the number of queued calls, a breakdown by call type, and the delay between calls, which defaults to three seconds. Name the campaign if you want to find it easily later, then launch it.

Progress updates as the campaign runs. The Stop button takes effect between calls, so a call already in progress finishes its conversation rather than being cut off.

Simulated campaigns use a much shorter delay between calls, so a large file completes in a few moments.
After the call

8. Call History

Every call, with its transcript and recording. Three tabs are available:

  • Personal, your own calls. This is the default view.
  • Colleagues, calls placed by others in your organisation.
  • Company, everything your organisation has placed.

Opening a call shows the transcript, the recording where one exists, and an Actions captured panel containing the disposition, a one-sentence summary, and everything the agent recorded during the conversation. This may include a promise to pay with the amount and date, a rescheduled appointment with its new time, which documents remain outstanding and when the customer undertook to send them, whether a service disruption was understood and which option was chosen, a dispute, a transfer to a colleague, or a corrected telephone number.

The transcript, summary and outcome arrive shortly after the call ends rather than instantly. A call that has just finished may briefly appear with nothing attached. Refresh the page and the detail will be present.
After the call

9. Write-back

Write-back sends the outcome of every completed call to your own system of record.

Available destinations:

  • Preview in-app, which writes nowhere and simply displays the rows. Use this first.
  • Webhook, posting JSON to any URL, with optional headers for authentication.
  • Enriched CSV, available as a download.
  • Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho and SQL databases, which require credentials.

What a row contains

A fixed set of columns is always present: who was called, the number, the call type, the outcome, a summary, the sentiment, the time, and the call reference. Beyond that, each row carries only what that particular call produced, so a documents chase is not padded out with empty payment columns.

One column is worth noting. next_step states in plain words what happens now.

next_step
"Pay 12500 by Friday"
"Booked for Tuesday at 10:30"
"Documents by Friday"
"Blocked: passport is with the visa office"
"Transferred to collections"
"Do not call again"

Alongside it you receive everything the agent captured, including promise amount and date, disputes, transfers, messages sent, corrected contact details, survey scores and comments, offer decisions with the reason given, and outstanding documents. The record also includes details extracted from the conversation automatically, such as the objection raised and the language the call was actually conducted in.

To control the columns yourself, configure a custom mapping. A column you specify is always written, whether or not that call populated it.

After the call

10. Analytics

How many calls were placed, to which countries, for how long, how they ended, and what the agent achieved. The same Personal, Colleagues and Company tabs apply, with Personal as the default.

Simulated calls are excluded by default. A switch is provided to include them, which is useful when demonstrating a populated dashboard without incurring telephony costs. Turn it off again before reporting any figure.

After the call

11. Scheduler

Calls and campaigns can be set to run unattended. Each schedule carries its own agent, market, timezone and calling window, so several may run against different countries at the same time without interfering with one another.

A schedule uses the agent profile as it stood when the schedule was created. Later edits to that profile do not alter a schedule already in place.

Schedules are visible to your whole organisation, so a colleague covering for you can see what is queued. Pausing and deleting remain with whoever created the schedule.

Configuration

12. Agent Builder

Compose an agent for a given industry and region, then save it. This is useful when preparing for a specific prospect, so that the configuration is ready next time rather than rebuilt from memory.

Saved configurations are visible across your organisation, so a team can work from one shared library. Editing and deleting remain with whoever saved the configuration.

Configuration

13. Company Profile

The full detail behind section 2. Load a preset, then edit it. The fields with the most noticeable effect on a call are the company name, the agent's name, the country and language, the form of address, and which use cases are enabled.

Further down there is a knowledge section: a short list of facts the agent may draw on, one per line. Write them as you want them spoken, since numbers and reference codes are read aloud.

Account and administration

14. Your calling allowance

Each account has a daily limit on live calling. The standard allowance is three calls or ten minutes of talk time per day, whichever is reached first. Simulated calls do not count towards it.

Bulk campaigns are disabled for partner accounts by default. Attempting to launch one produces the following message:

Bulk campaigns aren't switched on for your account just yet. Your daily allowance is 3 calls or 10 minutes of talk time, so calls are placed one at a time from the Single Call page. If you'd like to run a larger campaign, just ask your Streebo contact and we'll be glad to arrange it.

Bulk access and higher allowances are granted per account, and can be granted for a fixed period such as a single day or a week.

To request either, contact your Streebo representative. Allowances can be raised for a specific demonstration, with an expiry date attached.

16. Troubleshooting

"Backend not connected" appears across the top of the screen

The console cannot reach the server. On a hosted installation this usually means the service is restarting. Wait a moment and press Retry.

Every row in my file was held back

Check the "How your file was read" panel first. If the name or the telephone number is not listed there, they were not found, and no row can be called without both. If they are listed and rows are still held back, the Status column names the missing field for each row.

The agent did not mention the amount

That variable was empty for that row, and the agent speaks around an empty variable rather than reading out a blank. The "What we'll say" column in the analysis shows exactly what the agent was given.

A date was spoken as the wrong day

This is normally the day-first and month-first question described in section 6. Add one date later than the 12th to that column, or write dates in the form 2026-09-11.

The call ended sooner than expected

Live calls are limited to the talk time remaining on your account for that day, so the last call within an allowance may be cut short.

A number I have called before is being skipped

If it was marked do-not-call during an earlier conversation, that is honoured permanently across all future campaigns, and is shown as do-not-call (prior call).

Analytics show fewer calls than the history

Simulated calls are excluded from analytics unless the switch is enabled. The call history shows everything.

17. Scope and limitations

The following are outside the scope of the platform as it currently stands.

  • Inbound calls are not handled. A customer cannot return the call and reach the agent.
  • Numbers are not verified before dialling. An unreachable number is identified by calling it, and is recorded as no answer.
  • Calls are not retried automatically. A call that reaches voicemail is recorded and left. Re-dialling is a separate decision.
  • Data is not translated. The agent speaks the language set in the profile. Product names held in another language will be read in the profile language.
  • Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho and direct database write-back require credentials. The mapping is ready; the connection cannot be enabled from the console alone.
  • Dates cannot be disambiguated in a column where no date falls after the 12th. Where this occurs, a warning is shown rather than an assumption being made silently.
  • Missing values are not substituted. Where an amount or a date is absent, the call either proceeds without it or the row is held back with the missing column named.
